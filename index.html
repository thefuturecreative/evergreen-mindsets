About the Evergreen Creativity Project

Throughout the 2026–27 school year, you’ll explore how creative mindsets can be deliberately infused into the lessons and projects you already teach.

Your challenge: Select and integrate at least two different creative mindsets into an existing lesson or project. As you experiment, you’ll have opportunities to implement, observe, reflect, and refine your approach.

At the end of the year, you’ll share what you tried and what you learned, contributing to a collective Creativity Toolkit of real classroom examples available to all teachers at Evergreen.
